# Material recovery 1.3.116

Failed material recovery now permits a measured title-only repair without rewriting passing paragraphs. Empty body scope is explicit and the parser rejects unsolicited body changes. Missing product references and scene fidelity failures enter bounded source refresh/replanning under recovery policy 4; uncertain outputs, unknown owners and transport/authentication failures remain stop conditions. Confirmed early CAS failures retry only after fresh READY/FAILED and idle-image observations.

Final visual audits split supplemental comparison galleries into bounded 8 MiB requests while retaining exact mapped references, original resolution and every adverse verdict. Mandatory evidence that cannot fit remains blocked. Generation reference inputs may contain external seller artwork only when the intact selected product is identifiable; publication photographs still forbid editorial cards, frames and added descriptions.

Failure details are closed by default in material recovery results, current material blockers, history rows and both Sites recovery panels. Status counts/progress remain visible. Pending items are not counted as failures; native details controls permit keyboard opening and do not force opening on polling.

Verified offline: root and Sites TypeScript, relevant ESLint (existing warnings only), title-only and generation-reference tests, blocked material repair (161), material API (33), desktop display (6), Sites recovery (14), draft reliability, natural photography, final audit (146), reference audit (66) and selected gallery comparison (61).
