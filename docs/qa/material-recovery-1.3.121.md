## Corrected behavior and retained gates

- Approval errors retain legacy error strings and add structured audit failures. The server records exact section/node/path/SHA/context and the whole quoted photo assertion. Existing rejected pixels can be diagnostically re-audited before treating their slot as a fresh generation request.
- Only a sole photo-description mismatch with favorable identity, option and format findings can authorize one exact sentence removal. The server accepts finding keys, never caller-supplied verdicts or replacement prose. It checks the exact saved body, rendered paragraphs, manuscript and image bytes, limits the removed fraction, revalidates text quality, and requires a fresh audit of the same pixels before approval. Numeric/specification assertions, general product descriptions, partial quotes, identity/format defects and unknown/cancelled results do not authorize automatic deletion. Derived `specDraft`/`specValidation` caches are invalidated; `postSpec` source facts remain intact.
- A saved shopping draft's compulsory `seller-original` slot is bound first, with at most one source refresh. If the original remains missing, no new scene or hero generation is dispatched. This is a checkpoint after the manuscript exists, not a claim of complete pre-writing source validation. AI images never replace the original. Recovery policy 8 gives the changed source algorithm one bounded pass after policy 6/7 exhaustion; timestamps do not replenish the budget, and submission uncertainty/authentication/cancellation guards remain.
- Source candidate suitability and ranking are separate. A lower-ranked unreviewed candidate is not a proven pixel rejection. If the initial pool fails, one additional shortlist of at most 16 candidates receives renewed suitability review and the same final audit, with six final-audit rounds total. Genuine rejected pairs cannot re-enter. A product-appearance slot may use an intact white-background product photo; a literal action/feature claim still requires corresponding pixels. Wrong options, explanatory cards/frames, unverified identity and false scene claims continue to block.
- A product generation thread finishes immediately after its one requested image call; the host collects its native output. Observable SDK/limited same-thread rollout calls update a durable one-call budget and an observed second call aborts the turn. The SDK exposes no pre-dispatch admission hook; observed calls may already be submitted, and aliased/dynamic calls inside one opaque `exec` cannot be fully counted. This is an observation/stop guard, not a guarantee of one provider charge. Incomplete, cancelled or uncertain results are neither adopted nor retransmitted.
- Confirmed completed multiple outputs pass to the existing candidate-review coordinator for the exact recipe, selected snapshot and reference hashes, without another generation or browser fallback. Only one positively reviewed candidate can be selected automatically; multiple passing candidates remain ambiguous. Legacy copy-based receipts retain full workspace uniqueness checks. Only new valid host-collection receipts may ignore arbitrary helper photos.
- The photographic harness carries literal photo descriptions as untrusted article data, including descriptions beyond the old 480-character excerpt. It preserves the verified subject rather than inventing cream texture, kit contents, actions or results. Generator speculation cannot authorize text correction or approval. Body photos remain natural single photographs without explanatory text, frames, panels or collage.
