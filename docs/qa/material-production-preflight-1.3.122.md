## Corrected production flow

- Fact reading retains a separate bounded seller evidence pool of at most 24 decoded files. The publication photo/crop quotas stay unchanged. Wide panels and files excluded only by publication quota remain eligible for reading, never automatically eligible for publication. OCR keeps its eight-image limit. Vision uses at most two existing bounded batches without duplicate attachments and stops when evidence is sufficient, including when no tall crops exist.
- Independent-description checks recognize an echoed page title with its store suffix, without changing selected product/option identity. Real added numeric specifications and functions remain evidence. Failed intake writes a bounded `source-intake-diagnostics.json` sibling before throwing: `trusted:false`, `usableSnapshot:false`, sanitized source URLs, filtered/raw facts, stage results and source-file hashes. It is not a usable context or source snapshot. Reading-only temporary files are cleaned without deleting normal candidates.
- A new shopping manuscript or MCP context is prepared only after adequate final source facts and a mandatory seller original pass the selected-product/option photo review. A deterministic crop may recover actual seller pixels, but the same pixel review must approve it. This path never generates a substitute source photo. Its proof certifies visible product exterior suitability, not all hidden specifications or the complete purchased kit.
- The approved source snapshot, original path and SHA are shared by the writer, context export and subsequent composition. The original is attachment 1; other seller images are explicitly reading-only. Assembly starts with this same original and leaves the three generated scenes as future slots. Source/snapshot/option/byte drift blocks further preparation with a specific error code. Provider reconnects also recheck the original.
- MCP export persists an identical source copy outside collector temporary-file cleanup. Submission reads the server's saved proof and requires the same original bytes and full frozen snapshot rather than selecting another gallery photo. A different local cache fragment count does not rewrite frozen source metadata. New contexts missing their proof fail; legacy contexts still require fresh preflight. Approved saved publication and existing revision paths are preserved.
- Shared writing instructions distinguish `SOURCE_READING`, `VERIFIED_PUBLICATION_ORIGINAL` and `PLANNED_SCENE`. External writers may describe the actual photo only if that same original is attached; a URL/path/metadata alone cannot establish a scene. Catalog choices or common cuts plus weight do not establish inclusion in one selected kit. Confirmed product facts and descriptions of the actually assigned original remain allowed.
- Selected-product noun precedence classifies the Fullas lint remover as a handheld home appliance through Product9Canvas, composition and final photographic prompting. Pants/cardigan plans remain clothing. Natural single body photos continue to exclude explanatory frames, panels, text cards and collage.
- The internal browser recovery prompt budget is 6,500 characters so the mandatory photo contract and evidence fit; the primary budget remains 8,500. This is an application budget, not a claimed platform limit. Fixed mandatory overflow still fails rather than truncating required rules.
- Context-only log classification retains `SOURCE_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`, `IMAGE_SOURCE_BINDING_REQUIRED`, `PREWRITING_PHOTO_CHANGED` and `PREWRITING_SOURCE_CHANGED`, including when no draft result file exists. The preparation stage label now describes fact/original checking before writing.
