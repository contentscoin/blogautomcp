# Material recovery 1.3.117 follow-up

Live incremental repair under 1.3.116 identified two distinct defects. Catalog variants parsed from a title were treated as if they established a purchased option, and repeated matching trays were confused with multiple variants. Verified seller-feature claims were also omitted from image-audit context. The follow-up context distinguishes existing feature-based option interpretation from title candidates, carries only seller-feature claims with evidence links, and preserves rejection of real option conflicts, unresolved identity and unsupported photo descriptions. A photograph explicitly described as containing different forms of persimmon still requires those different forms to be visible.

Dalba's failed thumbnail was newly created by a local compositor directly from seller artwork containing a circular inset and gift promotion. The generation-reference allowance was valid only for input, but this thumbnail route published that input artwork. Final audit correctly rejected it. A subsequent recovery check counted only missing body slots and failed to repair the thumbnail-only defect. The corrective implementation must generate a fresh reference-guided photographic background, keep its review separate from final headline verification and include rejected thumbnails in bounded incremental recovery.

Image recovery policy 5 identifies the changed algorithm for a single fresh bounded attempt on previously exhausted evidence. It does not retry uncertain output, cancellation, authentication or transport failure. The CI contract now includes the Product 9Canvas checks; two obsolete assertions were updated to the existing natural-photo scale contract without changing that production behavior.

Offline checks include root TypeScript, the failed-material and blocked-material UI/API suites, 159 final-audit scenarios, 74 mapped-reference scenarios, raw/final-thumbnail lineage and tampering gates, thumbnail-only bounded recovery, missing/damaged/ambiguous hero planning and natural-photo migration. Source-only requests never start thumbnail generation. Normal photo thumbnails remain untouched; a missing hero is a creation target with an explicit slot, and uncertain or ambiguous replacement targets stay blocked. A pre-existing body bound to an earlier scene hero retains its anchor guard; none of the 14 live recovery targets has this legacy anchor binding.

The release is installed and the four-product live follow-up has completed. Sites version 49 and Windows 1.3.116 deployment and preservation evidence remain in `material-recovery-1.3.116.md`.
