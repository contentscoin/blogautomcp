# Copy and layout rules

## Copy hierarchy

| Layer | Purpose | Limit | Example |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Product label | Identify the product | 18 Korean characters | `에어젯 울트라 드라이기` |
| Headline | Express one reader decision | 12 Korean characters | `무게·바람 체크` |
| Supporting line | Name 2 to 3 verified checks | 22 Korean characters | `구성·소음·휴대성` |
| Badge | Optional context only | 8 Korean characters | `구매 체크` |
| CTA | Neutral next action | 10 Korean characters | `장단점 보기` |

Prefer nouns and check-oriented language. Copy should help a reader decide, not simulate personal experience.

## Intent mapping

- compare: `차이점 체크`, supporting line with verified comparison axes.
- feature-check: name the strongest verified feature, then 2 to 3 related checks.
- value-check: `가격·구성 체크`; use a numeric price only when confirmed and current.
- problem-solution: state the buying problem, not a guaranteed result.
- usage-fit: `어떤 상황에 맞을까`; name verified usage conditions.

## Image selection

Prefer a high-resolution gallery or representative product photo. Reject review photos, people whose consent is unknown, logos or icons, event banners, screenshots, delivery tables, and images below 500px on either side. Preserve the original aspect and avoid stretching.

## Layout

- Use a 1600x900 canvas with at least 56px outer safety margin.
- Allocate roughly 45 percent to copy and 55 percent to the product, adapting to the source photo's visual weight.
- Keep a dark translucent copy panel or verified high-contrast quiet area behind text.
- Product label is smaller than the headline. Supporting copy and CTA are subordinate.
- Check the result at approximately 320px wide; the product and headline must remain identifiable.

## QA

- The chosen photo is the actual target product.
- No product feature was hallucinated or visually altered.
- Korean copy is exact and not clipped.
- The headline communicates one decision.
- Price, discount, rating, ranking, and experience claims have explicit evidence.
- The thumbnail is the first image in the planned post.
